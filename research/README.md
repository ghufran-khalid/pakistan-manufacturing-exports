# Pakistan Industrialization research series

The Pakistan Industrialization research series is related work by Ghufran Khalid examining Pakistan’s industrial structure, manufacturing composition and broader structural transformation. The standalone [interactive dashboard](../dashboard/index.html) complements this written research.

2. What Does Pakistan Manufacture?
3. Pakistan’s Export Structure and Industrial Competitiveness
