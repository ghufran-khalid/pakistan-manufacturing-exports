# Version 0.1.0 — unpublished candidate

Pakistan Manufacturing and Export Competitiveness. This release presents the dashboard as a standalone data project that complements the broader Pakistan Industrialization research series.

Period: 2000–2024. Six sections and eight charts cover orientation, composition, concentration, industry RCA, world share and ECI, plus methodology. Technology and product explorers are omitted pending redistribution clearance. No research values changed. Growth Lab/Atlas and BLS/FRED sources are attributed. Aggregation sensitivity, RCA interpretation, constant-price and ECI scope limitations remain visible. No GitHub publication or Pages activation has occurred.

## Public-facing refinements

Simplified terminology, accessible technical-concept explanations, chart takeaways, and approved author information have been added. No research metrics or underlying data were changed.
