03 / Diversification

How concentrated are exports?

These measures show whether Pakistan’s manufactured exports are concentrated in a small number of products or spread more broadly across the export basket.

Loading…

Takeaway: Detailed-product concentration fell over the period, although the result changes when products are grouped more broadly.

Note: The top-5 and top-10 measures show the share of manufactured exports accounted for by the largest detailed products in each year. The concentration index (HHI) is also shown at detailed and broader product levels. Full methodology.

Source: Growth Lab / Atlas; project calculations.

What does CR5 mean?

The combined share of manufactured exports accounted for by the five largest detailed export products in each year.

What does CR10 mean?

The combined share of manufactured exports accounted for by the ten largest detailed export products in each year.

What does HHI mean?

Measures how concentrated exports are across products. Higher values mean a larger share of exports is concentrated in fewer products.

Why does product detail matter?

Combining products changes their shares and the squared-share sum. Over 2000–2024, the detailed-product index (SITC4) falls while the broader-product index (SITC3) rises. They describe different levels of detail. No antitrust thresholds apply, and a lower index is not automatically economically better.

04 / Comparative advantage

Where does Pakistan specialize?

Revealed comparative advantage shows the products and industries that Pakistan exports more intensively than the world average.

Loading…

Takeaway: Pakistan’s strongest revealed export specialization remains concentrated in textiles and apparel.

Note: RCA above 1 indicates that Pakistan exports an industry more intensively than the world average. The comparison uses three-year averages for 2000–02 and 2022–24, based on available annual observations. RCA does not by itself measure productivity. Full methodology.

Source: Growth Lab / Atlas; project calculations.

What does RCA mean?

RCA compares how important a product is in Pakistan’s exports with how important it is in world trade. A value above 1 means Pakistan exports that product more intensively than the world average. RCA reflects observed trade specialization. It does not by itself measure productivity or technological capability.

The product-level RCA explorer is not included in this release.

What does ECI mean?

The Economic Complexity Index summarizes how diverse a country’s exports are and how widely those products are exported by other countries. Higher values indicate a more complex export basket.

The Economic Complexity Index and rank cover the wider export basket, not just manufacturing. Ubiquity means how widely a product is exported by other countries.
